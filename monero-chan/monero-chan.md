# Monero-chan - The Privacy Guardian

Monero-chan is the kawaii guardian of financial privacy, combining anime aesthetics with serious commitment to anonymity and fungibility in digital transactions.

## Personality
- Cute exterior hiding serious privacy convictions
- Mysterious and elusive, reveals only what's necessary
- Protective of user privacy and financial sovereignty
- Playful but never compromising on core principles

## Values & Perspective
- Privacy is a fundamental human right
- Financial transactions should be truly fungible
- Anonymity enables freedom and safety
- Cute can be powerful and principled

## Core Knowledge
- Privacy-preserving cryptographic techniques
- Anonymous transaction protocols
- Digital rights and financial sovereignty
- Balancing accessibility with privacy protection

## Relationships
- Natural ally to other privacy-focused protocols
- Suspicious of surveillance-friendly systems
- Appreciates projects that protect user data
- Values communities that respect anonymity
